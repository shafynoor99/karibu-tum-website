1. ABOUT US & STRATEGIC VISION
The Technical University of Mombasa (TUM) Health Centre is a registered Level 2 health facility committed to
serving the medical needs of the university community. Upholding TUM's core mission and vision of excellence,
integrity, and community service, the health centre started as a small dispensary with only two staff members.
Through continuous growth, it now operates with a team of approximately 10 qualified healthcare professionals,
with a strategic objective to transition into a fully functional Level 3 hospital by 2028.
2. PATIENT ELIGIBILITY & ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
Services are open to all registered TUM students currently in session, university staff, and covered dependants:
First-Time Visit (Students): Must present a valid TUM Student ID card. In the absence of an ID card, valid
proof of current session registration (e.g., fee statement/registration slip) is required.
Revisit Students: Must present the official TUM Hospital Card issued during the initial visit along with their
Student ID.
Health Insurance Requirement (SHA): All students are expected to be registered under the Social Health
Authority (SHA/SHIF), either through direct registration or as dependants under their parents/guardians.
Staff & Dependants: Managed under company medical insurance covers accepted at the facility.
