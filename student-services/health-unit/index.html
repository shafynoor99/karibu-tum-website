Health Unit

Primary healthcare, clinical services and wellness support for the TUM community.

Contact & SupportAbout this service

About the service

The Technical University of Mombasa Health Centre is a registered Level 2 medical facility serving the University community. From its beginnings as a small dispensary, it has grown to a team of approximately 10 healthcare professionals, with a strategic objective to transition to a Level 3 hospital by 2028.

The Health Services Department provides primary care to eligible students, staff and covered dependants at the Main Campus in Mombasa and through a satellite clinic at the Kwale Campus Extension.

How to access this service

Students attending for the first time should bring a valid TUM Student ID or proof of current-session registration, such as a fee statement or registration slip. Returning students should bring their TUM Hospital Card and Student ID. Students are expected to be registered with SHA/SHIF, directly or as a dependant. Staff and dependants are served under medical insurance covers accepted by the facility.

Emergency: For emergencies after 5:00 PM or on weekends, call the Health Centre In-Charge, Dr. Rerimoi, on 0721 848 952. You can also contact the TUM Security Officer on duty, who will alert the healthcare provider on call. For life-threatening emergencies, seek emergency assistance immediately.

Health Centre information

Care, eligibility and support available to the TUM community.

Facility and eligibility

Registered Level 2 medical facility, with a strategic objective to transition to Level 3 by 2028.

Who can use the service

  • Registered TUM students currently in session.
  • University staff and covered dependants.

What students should bring

  • First visit: valid TUM Student ID, or proof of current-session registration such as a fee statement or registration slip.
  • Revisit: TUM Hospital Card issued at the first visit and Student ID.
  • Students are expected to be registered under SHA/SHIF, either directly or as dependants under a parent or guardian.

Clinical services

  • Triage and nursing care, including preliminary vital signs, temperature and blood-pressure checks.
  • Outpatient consultation, diagnosis and treatment by Clinical Officers and Clinical Assistants.
  • Consultations with a visiting Gynecological Consultant.
  • Registered pharmacy services; if a medicine is out of stock, an official prescription is provided for external purchase.
  • Basic diagnostic investigations and clinical laboratory tests.
  • Wound dressing and minor procedures for minor trauma, burns and surgical dressings.
  • TB screening, monitoring and treatment referral.
  • Voluntary, confidential HIV Testing and Counselling Services (HTS), including pre- and post-test counselling and wellness guidance.

Patient care pathway

  1. Registration and SHA check
  2. Triage and preliminary care
  3. Clinician consultation and diagnosis
  4. Pharmacy, laboratory or HTS service as needed
  5. Discharge or referral to a Level 3 or 4 facility

Accepted insurance partners

  • Social Health Authority (SHA)
  • UAP Old Mutual
  • AAR Insurance
  • Madison Insurance
  • Jubilee Health
  • First Assurance

Referral hospitals

  • Tudor Sub-County Hospital
  • Coast General Teaching & Referral Hospital (CGTRH)
  • Nearby Level 3 or 4 facilities near the student’s residence

After-hours support

After 5:00 PM, weekends and public holidays, call the In-Charge, Dr. Rerimoi, at 0721 848 952, or contact the TUM Security Officer on duty to reach the healthcare provider on call.

Service limitation

Maternal, antenatal and delivery services are not offered on-site. Referrals are issued for specialized reproductive health services.
