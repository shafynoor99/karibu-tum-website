Hostels & Accommodation

Find safe, affordable and student-friendly accommodation while you pursue your academic goals.

Contact & SupportAbout this service

About the service

The Accommodation Section provides clean, safe and student-friendly hostel accommodation to support students during their studies. On-campus bed spaces are limited, and the University also assists students to identify private hostels near campus.

Priority consideration for on-campus accommodation is given to students with special needs and international students, in consultation with the Office of the Dean of Students.

How to access this service

Reserve a bed through the University’s Online Room Reservation Portal. After a successful reservation, pay the accommodation fee through the e-Citizen platform. For private hostel options near the University, contact the Accommodation Section for assistance.

At a glance

Location
Accommodation Office: near the Dining Hall. Janitors’ Office: next to the Health Unit and Block SE (Umoja), Ground Floor.
Resident support
Resident students are supported day and night. During official working hours, visit the Accommodation Office. After hours, hostel Janitors supervise the hostels and attend to urgent matters and student grievances, including weekends and public holidays.

Accommodation information

Rates, support and services for students and visiting groups.

Our vision

To become a model accommodation services unit among institutions of higher learning in Kenya.

Our mission

To provide high-quality, affordable and reasonably priced accommodation to students and visiting groups.

Student accommodation rates

  • Category 1KSh 10,000 per student per semester
  • Category 2KSh 6,750 per student per semester

Rates cover accommodation only.

Accommodation for visitors

KSh 350–500 per person per night during holiday breaks. This rate covers accommodation only.

Services offered

  • Maintaining cleanliness and hygiene in hostels and surrounding areas.
  • Helping students arrange private hostel accommodation.
  • Providing friendly, experienced and supportive accommodation staff.
  • Providing continuous hostel supervision to support a safe and conducive living environment.

Hostel notice

Cooking is strictly prohibited in the hostels.

A cafeteria is available within the University compound, offering meals on a pay-as-you-eat basis.
