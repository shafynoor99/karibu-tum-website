# TECHNICAL UNIVERSITY OF MOMBASA
**Student Service Department**  
**ACCOMMODATION SERVICES**

## MESSAGE FROM THE HEAD OF ACCOMMODATION SECTION

Welcome to the Technical University of Mombasa (TUM)!

The Accommodation Section is committed to providing safe, affordable, clean and student-friendly accommodation that makes students feel at home while pursuing their academic goals.

## Accommodation Booking and Payment

Accommodation payments are made through the e-Citizen platform following a successful bed reservation through the University's Online Room Reservation Portal.

Our on-campus accommodation capacity is limited. However, priority consideration is given to students with special needs and international students, in consultation with the Office of the Dean of Students.

In addition to on-campus accommodation, the Accommodation Section assists students in identifying private hostels located near the University to ensure access to convenient and alternative accommodation options.

## Categories and Rates of Accommodation

The University offers two categories of student accommodation:

1. Kshs. 10,000 per student per semester.
2. Kshs. 6,750 per student per semester.

All the above charges cover accommodation only.

**Important Notice:** Cooking is strictly prohibited in the hostels. However, a cafeteria is available within the University compound, offering meals on a pay-as-you-eat basis.

## Accommodation for Visitors

The University also provides accommodation services to visitors and visiting groups during holiday breaks at a rate of Kshs. 350 to Kshs. 500 per person per night. This rate covers accommodation only.

## Our Vision

To become a model accommodation services unit among institutions of higher learning in Kenya.

## Our Mission

To provide high-quality, affordable and reasonably priced accommodation services to our primary clients, namely students and visiting groups.

## Services Offered

The Accommodation Section provides the following services:

- Maintenance of cleanliness and hygiene in hostels and their surrounding areas.
- Assistance with arrangements for private hostel accommodation.
- Provision of friendly, experienced and supportive accommodation staff to attend to students’ needs.
- Continuous hostel supervision and support to ensure a safe and conducive living environment.

## Office Operations and Student Support

The Accommodation Section provides continuous support to resident students throughout the day and night.

During official working hours, students are served through the Accommodation Office. After official working hours, hostel Janitors take charge of hostel supervision and attend to urgent matters and student grievances that may arise during the evenings, nights, weekends and public holidays.

## Office Locations

- **Janitors' Office:** Located next to the Health Unit and Block SE (Umoja), Ground Floor.
- **Accommodation Office:** Located near the Dining Hall.

## Our Commitment

We are committed to ensuring that every student enjoys a comfortable, safe, clean and conducive living environment that supports academic excellence and personal development.

We welcome you to the Technical University of Mombasa and wish you a comfortable and successful stay!
