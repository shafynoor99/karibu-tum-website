# TUM Mental Health and Well-Being Centre

## TUM Guidance and Counseling Centre

### Vision
To be a leading centre of excellence in promoting holistic mental health and well-being for students, staff, and the wider community.

### Mission
To foster psychological resilience and well-being through prevention, early intervention, counseling, crisis response, education, research, and community engagement.

### Core Services

#### Mental Wellness & Promotion
- Awareness campaigns, workshops and digital resources
- Peer Wellness Educators and student support

#### Counseling Services
- Confidential, professional, and compassionate mental health care
- Available for individuals, couples, families and groups

#### HIV, SRH and GBV
- Confidential HIV testing and counseling
- Sexual Reproductive Health and family planning support
- GBV crisis response, trauma therapy and referral pathways

#### Learning & Research
- Evidence-based applied research
- Insights on psychological trends for students and staff

> We listen, We don’t judge.
