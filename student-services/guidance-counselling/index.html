Guidance & Counselling

Confidential mental health and wellbeing support for students, staff and the wider community.

Contact & SupportAbout this service

About the service

The TUM Mental Health and Well-Being Centre promotes holistic psychological wellbeing across the University community. Its work includes prevention, early intervention, counselling, crisis response, education, research and community engagement.

The Centre provides confidential, professional and compassionate support. Reaching out for help is a sign of strength, and you do not need to wait until things become overwhelming.

How to access this service

Book a virtual or in-person session through the TUM Mental Health and Well-Being Centre website. For support by phone or email, use the contact details on this page.

Emergency: If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call the Kenya emergency numbers 999 or 112.

Mental Health and Well-Being Centre

We listen, We don’t judge.

Our vision

To be a leading centre of excellence in promoting holistic mental health and wellbeing for students, staff and the wider community.

Our mission

To foster psychological resilience and wellbeing through prevention, early intervention, counselling, crisis response, education, research and community engagement.

Our approach

  • Student- and staff-centred care.
  • Prevention and early intervention.
  • Crisis response and engagement.
Your Mental Health Matters. Hakuna shame comrade aki struggle na Mental Health issues. Seeking help is strength.

Mental wellness and promotion

  • Awareness campaigns, workshops and digital resources.
  • Peer Wellness Educators and student support.

Counselling services

  • Confidential, professional and compassionate mental health care.
  • Counselling for individuals, couples, families and groups.

HIV, sexual and reproductive health, and GBV

  • Confidential HIV testing and counselling.
  • Sexual and reproductive health and family-planning support.
  • Gender-based violence crisis response, trauma therapy and referral pathways.

Learning and research

  • Evidence-based applied research.
  • Insights on psychological trends for students and staff.
