Office of the Dean of Students

The Office of the Dean of Students looks after student welfare at Technical University of Mombasa and works with other departments so you can study, live and stay well on campus.

About the Dean

Mr. Joel Oluoch Awino

Dean of Students

The Dean of Students heads the student services department, which is responsible for student welfare matters at Technical University of Mombasa. The Dean’s Office works closely with other service departments across the University.
