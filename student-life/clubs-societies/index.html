About Clubs & Societies

The Technical University of Mombasa (TUM) features a wide variety of student-led clubs, academic associations, tech communities, and religious groups that help students build practical skills, network, and engage in campus life.

How to take part

Check current registration lists, sign up for activities, or reach out through the TUM Clubs and Societies Office or campus student life portals. Contact the Dean’s Office for current information about active groups.

Prominent Clubs and Societies at TUM

Academic and Professional Clubs

  • Civil Engineering Club An active student chapter that partners with industry organizations like the Kenya National Highways Authority (KeNHA) Coast Region for site visits, mentorship, and practical training.

Tech and Innovation Communities

  • GDG on Campus TUM Part of the Google Developer Groups network, focusing on peer-to-peer learning around web development, Google Cloud, and open-source tech.
  • AWS Student Builder Group (AWS SBG) A student-led community focused on cloud computing, AI, security, and hands-on AWS projects.

Faith-Based and Fellowship Groups

  • Technical University of Mombasa Christian Union (TUMCU) A Christian student fellowship, alongside other campus ministry and outreach fellowships.
  • Muslim Students Association One of the campus faith-based student organizations, alongside other faith-based groups.

Social and Talent-Based Groups

  • Cultural, environmental, debate, and performing arts societies A diverse range of student groups for exploring shared interests, causes, and talents.
