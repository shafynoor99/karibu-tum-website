Sports & Recreation

Sport and recreation keep you healthy, build teamwork and give you a break from study.

Ask the Dean’s OfficeAbout this area

About Sports & Recreation

TUM students take part in a wide range of sports through the Kenya University Sports Federation (KUSF), including Coast Conference competitions, national championships and specialist events.

The 2026/2027 KUSF Coast Conference approved calendar includes events hosted at both TUM Main Campus and the Kwale Campus. Students Dan Kipchichir and Patience Mutheu are also attending the 10th Woodball World Cup Championship in Malaysia.

How to take part

For information about sports participation and the University teams, contact the TUM Sports Officer at sportsofficer@tum.ac.ke.
