Student Association

The Technical University of Mombasa Students Association (TUMSA) represents students, advocates for their welfare and provides a democratic channel for student participation.

Ask the Dean’s OfficeTUMSA Elections (opens in a new tab)

About Student Association

TUMSA is the students’ representative association at the Technical University of Mombasa. Guided by its Constitution, it works to represent students’ rights, interests and aspirations and to provide a democratic way to raise concerns and contribute to University life.

The Association brings students together while respecting different backgrounds, cultures, beliefs and abilities. Its work is guided by transparency, accountability, participation, integrity and social justice.

How to take part

Connect with your School, Institute, hall or campus representative; attend student meetings and forums; participate in elections; and contribute to approved welfare, sports, entertainment and community-service activities. Consult the TUMSA Constitution through the University or TUMSA for details of membership and governance.

Representation, welfare and participation

Key roles and responsibilities described in the TUMSA Constitution.

Student representation

TUMSA’s constitutional structures provide ways for students to share concerns and participate in decisions affecting student life.

  • The Student Council carries out leadership and day-to-day functions assigned by the Constitution and engages with relevant University offices on student affairs and welfare.
  • The TUMSA Congress is the Association’s supreme policy-making organ under the Constitution, bringing student representatives together for deliberation and policy decisions.
  • School and Institute representatives communicate students’ views and academic concerns. Other constituencies include halls of residence, satellite campuses, students with special needs and religious groups, as provided for in the Constitution.

Student welfare

TUMSA liaises with relevant University departments and offices on matters affecting students’ everyday welfare.

  • Accommodation and conditions in halls of residence and outside hostels.
  • Catering and access to affordable meals.
  • Health, hygiene, clean water and access to health services.
  • Coordination when students experience sickness or bereavement.

Inclusion and student activities

The Constitution provides for representation of students with special needs and promotes their integration into the University community. TUMSA can raise accessibility and inclusion concerns with the Association and appropriate University bodies.

TUMSA also supports student interaction through sports, tournaments, entertainment and approved activities. These provide opportunities for teamwork, talent development and community participation.

  • Encourage academic excellence, research, and the development of artistic and professional talents.
  • Promote freedom of expression, association, conscience and academic liberty within applicable laws and University regulations.
  • Build unity among students from different backgrounds and foster constructive relationships with University staff and the wider public.
  • Encourage community service and cooperation with TUM alumni.

Elections and accountability

TUMSA leadership and representation are established through constitutional processes, including elections. The Constitution provides for a University Electoral Commission to oversee specified student elections and procedures.

This page is an accessible overview based on the TUMSA Constitution (April 2020), not a replacement for the Constitution. Refer to the Constitution through the University or TUMSA for complete information on membership, rights, responsibilities and governance.

Your voice matters. Connect with your student representatives, take part in TUMSA activities and help make student life better for everyone.
