# TUMSA – Technical University of Mombasa Students Association

## About TUMSA

The Technical University of Mombasa Students Association (TUMSA) is the students’ representative association at the Technical University of Mombasa. It exists to represent students, promote their welfare, protect their rights and interests, and provide a democratic channel through which students can raise concerns and contribute to university life.

TUMSA brings students together around shared aspirations while respecting the diversity of their backgrounds, cultures, beliefs, and abilities. Its work is guided by the TUMSA Constitution and the principles of transparency, accountability, student participation, integrity, and social justice.

## What Does TUMSA Do?

TUMSA works to make student voices heard and to support a positive university experience. Its constitutional objectives include:

- **Representing students:** Advocating for students’ needs, interests, rights, and aspirations through democratic representation.
- **Promoting student welfare:** Raising and addressing concerns related to students’ social welfare and campus experience.
- **Supporting academic development:** Encouraging academic excellence, research, and the development of artistic and professional talents.
- **Promoting equality and inclusion:** Supporting fair gender representation and equitable participation in student affairs, including the interests of students with special needs.
- **Encouraging student freedoms:** Promoting students’ freedom of expression, association, conscience, and academic liberty, within applicable laws and university regulations.
- **Building unity:** Encouraging cooperation among students of different backgrounds, religions, and communities.
- **Supporting peaceful engagement:** Helping foster harmony among students and constructive relationships with university staff, management, and the wider public.
- **Promoting community service:** Encouraging students to take part in community service in line with the University’s objectives.
- **Encouraging alumni cooperation:** Supporting continued cooperation with former TUM students through the Alumni Association.

## Student Representation

TUMSA provides structures through which students can share their concerns and take part in decisions affecting student life.

### The Student Council

The Student Council carries out the leadership and day-to-day functions assigned to it by the Constitution. It works on student affairs and welfare and engages with relevant University offices and structures.

### The Congress

The TUMSA Congress is the Association’s supreme policy-making organ under the Constitution. It brings together student representatives from different parts of the University and provides a forum for deliberation, representation, and policy decisions.

### Student Representatives

Representatives help connect students with TUMSA. School and Institute representatives collect students’ views, communicate academic needs and concerns, and present them for consideration. Other representation structures include halls of residence, satellite campuses, special needs, religious groups, and other constituencies provided for in the Constitution.

## Student Welfare

Student welfare is an important part of TUMSA’s mandate. The Constitution provides for representation and coordination on matters that affect students’ everyday lives, including:

- Accommodation and conditions in halls of residence and outside hostels.
- Catering and access to affordable meals.
- Health, hygiene, and access to health services.
- Availability of clean water.
- Support and coordination when students experience sickness or bereavement.
- Welfare concerns raised by students and referred to the relevant University offices.

TUMSA works in liaison with relevant University departments and offices on these matters.

## Sports, Entertainment, and Student Activities

TUMSA supports student interaction and participation in activities that bring the University community together. Its constitutional structures provide for sports and entertainment coordination, including tournaments, sporting events, and approved entertainment activities.

These activities help encourage teamwork, healthy interaction, talent development, and a sense of community among students.

## Inclusion and Special Needs

TUMSA recognizes the importance of equitable participation in student affairs. The Constitution provides for representation of students with special needs and assigns responsibilities for promoting their integration into the University community.

This includes raising issues affecting students with disabilities, sharing information about relevant facilities and services, and advising the Association and appropriate University bodies on inclusion.

## Elections, Accountability, and Student Participation

TUMSA leadership and representation are established through constitutional processes, including elections. The Constitution provides for a University Electoral Commission to oversee specified student elections and electoral procedures.


## How Can Students Engage with TUMSA?

Students can take part in TUMSA by:

- Contacting their School, Institute, hall, or campus representatives about concerns and suggestions.
- Participating in student elections and other legitimate Association activities.
- Attending meetings and forums announced for students.
- Sharing ideas that can improve student welfare, academic life, inclusion, and campus community.
- Taking part in approved sports, entertainment, and community-service activities.
- Reading the TUMSA Constitution to understand membership rights, responsibilities, and Association structures.

## TUMSA Constitution

The TUMSA Constitution sets out the Association’s objectives, membership, leadership structures, representation, elections, meetings, responsibilities, and governance procedures. Students can consult the Constitution through the University or TUMSA website to learn more about how the Association operates.

## Our Commitment

TUMSA is founded on the idea that students should have a meaningful voice in matters that affect them. Through representation, welfare advocacy, participation, and cooperation, the Association seeks to promote a university community where students’ rights are respected, concerns can be raised, and their aspirations are represented.
